Terracotta Turtle-Shaped Vessel Lid (ฝาภาชนะดินเผารูปเต่า)

- Period / Dating: 12th-16th centuries Buddhist Era (approx. 7th-11th centuries CE)
- Material: Baked earthenware / Terracotta (Din Phao)
- Dimensions: Diameter: 18.2 cm | Height: 4.7 cm
- Discovery Location: Excavated from the archaeological site at Ban Jaleh, Yarang Ancient City, Pattani Province
- Current Repository: Regional Office of Fine Arts No. 11, Songkhla

Curatorial Description:
Sculpted from local baked earthenware, this circular lid is modeled in the naturalistic form of a flattened turtle (Fa Phachana Din Phao Rup Tao). The piece features a broad domed carapace, four short protruding feet scored with five linear incisions each to denote individual toes, and a protruding turtle head extending forward with a subtle tongue-out expression.

The upper surface of the carapace bears clear archaeological evidence of applied sculptural ornamentation:
- Miniature Sculptures: Two distinct scar marks on the upper shell mark where miniature baby turtle figurines were originally affixed before detaching over time.
- Rear Ornamentation: An additional scar mark at the rear edge indicates the original placement of another small decorative finial or miniature element of unidentified form.

Ritual & Cultural Significance:
Archaeological analysis indicates that this zoomorphic lid was specifically crafted to seal a consecrated earthenware deposit vessel containing auspicious items and sacred relics (watthu mongkhon). Such vessels were deposited within the structural foundations of religious monuments during auspicious groundbreaking and consecration rituals (kan wang roek).

In ancient South and Southeast Asian cosmology, the turtle embodies foundational stability, cosmic support, and protection against negative energies. Discovered within the monument complex of Ban Jaleh, this artifact provides tangible material evidence of the intricate foundation-laying rituals and skilled ceramic craftsmanship practiced within the ancient peninsular trade kingdom of Langkasuka.
