**Front View**

> Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 8th–9th century CE Dvaravati-style standing stone Buddha statue (*Phra Phuttharup Sila* from Yarang Ancient City). The Buddha stands upright in *samabhanga* atop a carved double-lotus pedestal (*bua khwam bua ngai*). Both hands are raised symmetrically at navel level, pressed close to the torso, displaying the *Vitarka Mudra* (gesture of teaching). Wearing sheer, body-clinging monastic robes covering both shoulders, with a broad U-shaped frontal hem curving down to the shins and an inner garment extending to the ankles. Broad, flat meditative face with downcast eyes, defined wide lips, elongated earlobes, large stylized hair curls, and a cylindrical rounded *ushnisha*. Weathered grey sandstone texture, ancient museum artifact, clean neutral beige studio background, soft museum directional lighting, sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
