**Front View**
Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 6th–7th century CE sandstone sculpture of a recumbent humped bull/cow (*Pratimakam Rup Wua Sila* from Yarang Ancient City). The cow rests in a crouching pose facing directly forward, with its front legs folded underneath its chest. Prominent rounded snout with defined nostrils, upright pointed ears, short smooth horns, and a subtle dewlap extending down the throat. The large dorsal hump rises clearly above the shoulder ridge behind the head. Smooth, unadorned carved sandstone surface with subtle ancient weathering and micro-texture. Clean neutral beige museum background, soft directional studio lighting, crisp 8k photorealistic detail.
