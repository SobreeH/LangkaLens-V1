**Front View**

Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 7th–8th century CE bronze Bodhisattva Maitreya statue (*Phra Phothisat Mettraiya Samrit* from Yarang, Pattani). The figure stands in a slender, graceful triple-bend pose (*tribhanga*). Both arms are broken off and missing below the shoulders. Features a serene meditative face, elongated earlobes, and a tall ascetic crown of matted hair (*Jatamukuta*) adorned at the front center with a miniature stupa. A sacred thread (*yajnopavita*) runs diagonally across the left shoulder across the chest, and a lower skirt-cloth (*sabong*) wraps around the hips, tied at the waist with a knotted sash (*ratprakot*). Mounted on a simple dark wooden block pedestal. Authentic weathered bronze with rich olive-green oxidized patina, clean neutral beige studio backdrop, directional museum spotlighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
