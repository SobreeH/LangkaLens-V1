**Stupa 1: Terracotta Stupa with Inverted-Bowl Dome and Ringed Spire (Catalog Item 12)**

**Front View**

Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 7th–9th century CE miniature terracotta stupa (*Sathup Din Phao* from Yarang Ancient City). The lower section features a stepped circular base supporting a narrow recessed waist (*thong mai*). Above rests an inverted-bowl hemispherical bell dome (*ong rakhang song o khwam*). Rising directly from the dome is an integrated conical spire composed of multiple stacked, segmented ring ridges (*plong chanai*) tapering upward to a pointed lotus-bud finial (*bua tum*). Aged buff and pale terracotta earthenware texture with authentic surface erosion and micro-cracks, clean neutral beige museum background, soft directional studio lighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.

**Stupa 2: Architectural Terracotta Stupa with Kudu Niches and Balustrade Windows (Catalog Item 14)**

**Front View**

Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 7th–9th century CE architectural terracotta stupa base excavated at Ban Jaleh No. 3, Yarang Ancient City. The cylindrical body is decorated with applied clay relief (*pan-pae*): a lower band of gear/dentil teeth supporting a central horseshoe-arch niche (*kudu*) flanked by round columns and a notched bracket arch. Directly adjacent to the kudu niche is a rectangular blind window panel with vertical balustrade bars (*lak krong mai*). The upper section features a downward-lotus moulding with false roof beam projections (*khue*), a waist frieze decorated with four-petaled square rosettes, and an upward-lotus rim. Traces of ancient red mineral pigment on pale buff baked clay, neutral beige backdrop, museum spotlighting, sharp 8k detail.

---

**Left Profile View**

Left side profile view (90-degree lateral angle) of the architectural terracotta stupa base. Showcases the relief depth of the applied architectural elements: the side elevation of the kudu horseshoe arch with columnar pilasters, rectangular balustrade window grilles, and protruding roof beam brackets. Stepped circular base profile below and flaring lotus mouldings above. Weathered buff-terracotta earthenware with red pigment traces settled in carved recesses, soft directional rim lighting, neutral background, photorealistic 8k detail.

---

**Right Profile View**

Right side profile photograph of the Yarang architectural miniature stupa. Lateral angle highlighting the repeating architectural frieze of alternating kudu blind arches and rectangular balustrade windows separated by slender cylindrical colonnettes. Protruding upper eaves supported by false beam rafters and the square four-petaled floral rosettes along the upper band. Antique earthenware clay texture with natural micro-wear and chipped edges, neutral studio setting, crisp 8k archival photography.

**Stupa 3: Water-Pot Stupa with 7-Tiered Chatra-valee Umbrella Spire (Catalog Items 13 & 23 / Typology 1.2 & 2.1)**

**Front View**

Frontal museum photograph of an ancient 7th–9th century CE baked clay stupa (*Sathup Din Phao* from Yarang Ancient City). Features a tall stepped circular pedestal base supporting a bulbous water-pot shaped bell dome (*ong rakhang song mo nam*). Mounted atop the dome is a tall conical spire composed of 7 hierarchical circular umbrella plates (*chatra-valee*) stacked along a central shaft, decreasing in size from bottom to top and crowned by an elongated pointed lotus-bud finial (*bua tum*). Fine-grained warm reddish-buff earthenware clay texture, clean neutral studio backdrop, directional museum lighting, sharp 8k photorealistic detail.

Matte reddish-buff terracotta texture with subtle historical tool markings, neutral beige background, soft rim lighting, ultra-detailed 8k resolution.
