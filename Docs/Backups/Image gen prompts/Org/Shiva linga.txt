**Front View**
Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 7th–8th century CE classic tripartite sandstone Shiva Linga (*Siwalueng Sila* from Yarang Ancient City). Monolithic vertical carving divided into three equal sections: a square base (*Brahma-bhaga*), an octagonal middle prism (*Vishnu-bhaga*), and a rounded egg-shaped cylindrical top (*Rudra-bhaga*). The front of the rounded top features a distinct raised vertical central ridge (*Brahmasutra*) flanked symmetrically by curved downward-sloping contour lines (*Parsvasutra*). Aged grey-brown sandstone texture with visible grain, subtle mineral erosion, and traces of reddish pigment/patina on the lower square base, centered on a clean neutral beige museum backdrop, soft directional studio lighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
