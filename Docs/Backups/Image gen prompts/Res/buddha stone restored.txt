**Front View**

> Frontal studio photograph of a fully restored 8th–9th century CE Dvaravati-style standing stone Buddha statue (*Phra Phuttharup Sila* from Yarang Ancient City) in prime, pristine original condition. The Buddha stands in an erect, balanced *samabhanga* stance atop an unblemished, crisply carved double-lotus pedestal (*bua khwam bua ngai*) with sharp blooming petals. Both hands are raised symmetrically to navel level and carved flush against the torso in the distinctive Yarang regional style, performing the *Vitarka Mudra* (gesture of teaching) with intact, delicately sculpted fingers. Draped in a sheer, body-hugging monastic robe (*civara*) covering both shoulders (*hom khlum*), featuring a fluid, unbroken U-shaped hem curving down to the shins and revealing an inner lower garment extending to the ankles. Flat, serene meditative face with unweathered contours, crisp downcast eyes, defined wide lips, elongated earlobes, neatly sculpted large hair curls, and a smooth cylindrical *ushnisha*. Polished fine-grained sandstone with a smooth satin finish and subtle warm mineral tones, clean neutral beige studio backdrop, soft directional museum spotlighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
