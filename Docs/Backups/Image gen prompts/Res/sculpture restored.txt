**Front View**

Frontal studio photograph of a fully restored 7th–8th century CE bronze Bodhisattva Maitreya statue (*Phra Phothisat Mettraiya Samrit* from Yarang, Pattani) in prime, unblemished original condition. The figure stands in a graceful, slender triple-bend pose (*tribhanga*) atop an integrated circular bronze lotus pedestal. Fully restored with both arms intact: the right hand is raised in the gesture of teaching/exposition (*vitarka mudra*), while the left hand holds a sacred ritual water vessel (*kundika*) resting gracefully beside the hip. Features a serene meditative face with elongated earlobes and an intricately braided ascetic crown (*jatamukuta*) centered at the front with a sharp, unbroken miniature stupa emblem. A delicate sacred cord (*yajnopavita*) crosses diagonally over the left shoulder across the bare chest, with a sheer lower skirt-cloth (*sabong*) secured at the waist by an ornate tied sash (*ratprakot*) looping down the thighs. Smooth, polished antique bronze with a warm, gleaming golden-brown metallic luster, completely free of green verdigris, corrosion, or fractures. Clean neutral beige studio background, soft museum directional spotlighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
