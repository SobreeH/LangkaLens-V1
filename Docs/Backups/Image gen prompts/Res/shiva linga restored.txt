**Front View**
Frontal studio photograph of a fully restored 7th–8th century CE canonical tripartite carved sandstone Shiva Linga (*Siwalueng Sila* from Yarang Ancient City) shown in prime, unblemished original condition. Monolithic vertical sculpture divided into three balanced, equal sections: a sharp geometric square base block (*Brahma-bhaga* representing Brahma), a pristine octagonal prismatic shaft (*Vishnu-bhaga* representing Vishnu) with clean beveled facets, and a smooth egg-shaped cylindrical dome (*Rudra-bhaga* representing Shiva). The front of the rounded top features crisp, undamaged relief lines with a raised vertical central ridge (*Brahmasutra*) passing over the apex, flanked symmetrically by sharp downward-curving contour lines (*Parsvasutra*). Honed fine-grained grey-buff sandstone with a smooth, unweathered satin finish and zero chipping, cracks, or discoloration, clean neutral beige museum studio backdrop, directional soft spotlighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
