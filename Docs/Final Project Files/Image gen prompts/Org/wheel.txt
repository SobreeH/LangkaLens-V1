**Front View**
Frontal studio photograph of an ancient 6th–7th century CE carved sandstone Dharmachakra (Wheel of the Law / *Thammachak Sila* from Yarang Ancient City). The circular wheel features 8 openwork spokes radiating outward from a smooth, circular central hub. The wheel is mounted directly atop a thick rectangular stone block, which rests upon a rounded, flattened spherical base pedestal. Weathered dark brownish-grey sandstone texture with visible grain, natural surface pitting, and micro-erosion. Clean neutral beige studio background, soft museum directional spotlighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.
