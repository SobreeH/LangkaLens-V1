Terracotta Stupa with Water-Pot Dome and 7-Tiered Chatra-valee Spire

- Dating / Period: 12th-14th centuries Buddhist Era (approx. 7th-9th centuries CE)
- Material: Fine-grained baked earthenware
- Discovery Location: Yarang Ancient City, Pattani Province
- Current Repository: Fine Arts Department Office No. 11, Songkhla
- Description: A modular votive stupa featuring a high stepped base that supports a bulbous, water-pot shaped bell dome (ong rakhang song mo nam). Its upper superstructure is composed of a separate conical spire featuring seven concentric umbrella discs (chatra-valee) stacked along a central shaft in decreasing size, terminating in an elongated lotus-bud finial (bua tum). The seven tiered umbrellas represent the ascending celestial realms of Buddhist cosmology, embodying the devotional ceramic arts of the ancient maritime kingdom of Langkasuka.
