**Stupa 1: Restored Terracotta Stupa with Inverted-Bowl Dome and Ringed Spire (Catalog Item 12)**

**Front View**

Frontal architectural photograph of a fully restored 7th–9th century CE miniature terracotta stupa (*Sathup Din Phao* from Yarang Ancient City) shown in prime, newly fired condition. The lower section features a crisp stepped circular base supporting a sharply defined recessed waist (*thong mai*). Above rests a smooth, unblemished hemispherical inverted-bowl bell dome (*ong rakhang song o khwam*). Rising seamlessly from the dome is a complete, fully intact conical spire composed of evenly spaced horizontal ring ridges (*plong chanai*) tapering upward to a pointed lotus-bud finial (*bua tum*). Smooth warm buff-terracotta earthenware surface with subtle kiln firing blushing and zero cracks, clean neutral beige museum backdrop, soft directional studio lighting, ultra-sharp 8k photorealistic detail.

**Stupa 2: Restored Architectural Terracotta Stupa with Kudu Niches and Red Pigment (Catalog Item 14)**

**Front View**

Frontal architectural photograph of a fully reconstructed and restored 7th–9th century CE architectural terracotta stupa from Ban Jaleh No. 3, Yarang Ancient City, in prime original condition. The cylindrical body is decorated with fresh, sharp applied clay reliefs (*pan-pae*): a lower band of gear/dentil teeth supporting a horseshoe-arch niche (*kudu*) flanked by slender round columns and a notched bracket arch. Directly adjacent is a blind window panel with vertical balustrade bars (*lak krong mai*). The upper section features a downward-lotus moulding with false roof beam projections (*khue*), a waist frieze with crisp four-petaled square rosettes, and an upward-lotus rim supporting a reconstructed dome and 7-tier umbrella spire. Vibrant ancient red mineral pigment accents washed over pale buff baked clay, neutral beige backdrop, museum spotlighting, sharp 8k detail.
