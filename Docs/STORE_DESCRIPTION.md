## 📜 Long Description (< 1,500 Characters)

*Copy and paste the text block below directly into the Meta Developer Dashboard **Long Description** field:*

```text
Step into the history of Southern Thailand with LangkaLens (ลังกาเลนส์), an immersive VR and MR cultural heritage app for Meta Quest.

Journey into the past to explore Ancient Yarang City (Ban Jalae, Pattani Province)—a key trading port and religious center of the Dvaravati and Srivijaya eras. Whether visiting remotely or on-site, LangkaLens connects archaeology with spatial computing.

🏛️ KEY FEATURES:

🌐 360° Photosphere Ruins Tour
Explore excavated sites (BN2, BN3, BN8, BW9) with spatial waypoints, minimap navigation, and interactive 3D ghost reconstructions overlaying ancient structures onto real-world ruins.

🏺 Virtual Museum & Artifact Inspection
Examine digitized relics up close. Use Touch controllers or hand tracking to grab and rotate ancient stupas and votive tablets with bilingual (Thai/English) descriptions and audio narration.

👓 On-Site Mixed Reality (MR) Blending
Designed for on-site visitors using Meta Quest passthrough to blend physical ruins with full-scale virtual reconstructions, supported by physical alignment tools and safety boundary tracking.

🤖 Gemini AI Cultural Heritage Guide
Converse with an AI guide powered by Google Gemini via voice or a bilingual spatial keyboard to learn about trade routes, art styles, and history.

🎮 Supported on Quest 2, 3, 3S, & Pro with Thai/English localization.
```
