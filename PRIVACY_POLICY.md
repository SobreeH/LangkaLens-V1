# Privacy Policy for LangkaLens

**Last Updated:** September 30, 2026  
**Effective Date:** September 30, 2026  

Welcome to **LangkaLens (ลังกาเลนส์)** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to the LangkaLens virtual reality (VR) and mixed reality (MR) application available on the Meta Horizon Store for Meta Quest devices (including Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S, and Pro).

We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy provides a clear explanation of what data is processed, how it is used, and how you can exercise your data rights, in compliance with Meta Quest Virtual Reality Check guidelines (**VRC.Privacy.1**, **VRC.Privacy.2**, **VRC.Privacy.3**, and **VRC.Privacy.4**), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

---

## 1. Information We Process and Collect (VRC.Privacy.2)

LangkaLens is designed as an educational cultural heritage experience that prioritizes user privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. 

### A. Data Processed Locally On-Device
- **Spatial Tracking & Headset Movement**: Head, hand, and controller 6DOF spatial tracking data is processed locally in real-time by Meta Quest system software (Horizon OS) to render the virtual environment, handle locomotion, and support hand interactions. We do not record, store, or transmit your physical movement coordinates.
- **Mixed Reality & Passthrough Camera Feed**: When using Mixed Reality mode, the passthrough camera sensors are managed strictly by Meta Horizon OS on-device. LangkaLens does **not** capture, record, store, or transmit any raw camera images, video streams, or spatial mesh reconstructions of your physical surroundings.
- **Local Application Preferences**: Your selected interface language (Thai or English), audio volume, and comfortable viewing preferences are saved locally on your Meta Quest headset storage via standard application settings.

### B. Features Requiring External Processing (AI Cultural Guide)
- **Voice Dictation & Text Queries**: If you choose to interact with the in-app Gemini AI Cultural Heritage Guide via voice or the virtual keyboard, your spoken audio is converted to text locally on the device, and the resulting text query is transmitted securely (via HTTPS/TLS) to the Google Gemini Generative Language API solely to generate contextual archaeological answers. No personal account credentials, real names, or identity markers are attached to these requests.

### C. Meta Horizon Platform Data
- **Entitlement Checks**: When you launch LangkaLens, the app communicates with the Meta Horizon Platform SDK to confirm your software entitlement. No private profile information (such as friend lists, payment methods, or real names) is gathered or stored on our servers.

---

## 2. How We Use User Data (VRC.Privacy.3)

Any data processed by LangkaLens is used strictly for the following operational and educational purposes:
1. **Delivering Core App Functionality**: Enabling you to explore 360° ruins, inspect 3D museum artifacts, and view mixed reality reconstructions.
2. **Providing the Conversational AI Guide**: Generating real-time, contextually accurate historical and archaeological responses to user questions.
3. **Saving User Preferences**: Remembering your preferred language (Thai or English) across sessions.
4. **Platform Verification**: Verifying valid app ownership and compatibility through the Meta Horizon Store.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, behavioural tracking, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Retention, and Sharing

- **Zero Third-Party Data Sales**: We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
- **Third-Party Service Providers**: The in-app conversational guide transmits user query text to Google Gemini APIs strictly for generating responses. Processing is governed by Google Cloud's data privacy and security commitments.
- **No Remote Database Storage**: We do not maintain external remote servers holding player databases, tracking logs, or user profiles.

---

## 4. User Data Deletion and Your Privacy Rights (VRC.Privacy.4)

All users, regardless of geographic location or jurisdiction, have the right to request the deletion of any data that has been collected, stored, or processed in connection with LangkaLens.

### How to Request Data Deletion:
Because LangkaLens does not store account profiles or personal databases on external servers, resetting your locally stored preferences is as simple as:
- **Local Data**: You can erase all locally stored settings at any time by uninstalling the application from your Meta Quest library, or by navigating to **Settings > Storage > LangkaLens > Clear Data** on your Meta Quest headset.

### Submitting a Formal Data Deletion Request or Inquiry:
If you have any questions, wish to verify whether any associated data exists, or wish to submit a formal data deletion request:
1. Send an email to our designated contact address: **[sobree.h@gmail.com](mailto:sobree.h@gmail.com)**
2. Use the subject line: **"LangkaLens - Data Deletion Request"**
3. Include your Meta Horizon username or approximate request details.

**Guarantee:**
- We do **not** charge any fee for data deletion requests.
- All validated deletion requests will be reviewed and completed within **30 calendar days**.
- If no identifiable data is held, you will receive confirmation that no personal records exist on our systems.
